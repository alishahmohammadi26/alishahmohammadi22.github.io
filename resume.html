Summary

Data and AI leader with more than ten years in regulated life-sciences R&D, including four-plus years at Takeda building enterprise data governance, FAIR data, and AI-enablement capabilities across research domains. Treats data trust as an operating system rather than a manual review cycle: governance policy expressed as automated controls embedded in platforms, ownership and stewardship models that hold across technical and business domains, and agentic AI that performs first-pass curation, quality checking, and lineage capture with human approval retained where accountability requires it. Leads multidisciplinary teams of engineers, data scientists, and process specialists, partners across Product, Engineering, Architecture, QA, Security, and Research in a large matrixed organization, and translates GxP and regulatory requirements into scalable standards and trusted data products.
