Professional Summary

Applied AI engineer with 10+ years in pharmaceutical R&D building production-grade systems where data quality directly determines scientific conclusions. I ship typed, tested Python — MCP servers that expose scientific data to AI agents as deterministic callable tools, multi-agent LangGraph pipelines with human-in-the-loop controls, and evaluation harnesses that measure correctness and catch hallucinations before they reach a scientist. I also deployed FAIR Studio (Pistoia Alliance), an industry-wide platform operationalizing FAIR data assessment at scale. The thesis: trustworthy data infrastructure isn’t a nice-to-have for frontier AI in life sciences — it’s the foundation everything else stands on.
