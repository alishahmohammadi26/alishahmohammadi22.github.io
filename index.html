About
Chemical Engineer. Data Scientist. Digital Leader.

I’m a Chemical Engineer turned digital leader with a Ph.D. from Queen’s University and 10+ years of experience spanning pharmaceutical R&D, data science, and AI/ML systems. I currently lead FAIR Data Strategy and Digital Connectivity at Takeda Pharmaceutical, where I architect enterprise-scale governance platforms and deploy production Agentic AI systems.

My work bridges the gap between cutting-edge AI and real-world pharmaceutical applications — from building digital twins for continuous manufacturing to deploying multi-agent systems for ontology curation to partnering with MIT, BYU, Brown, and Purdue on Physics-Informed Neural Networks.

I’m passionate about making data trustworthy, accessible, and AI-ready at scale — and building the infrastructure that lets scientists focus on science rather than data wrangling.
