FAIR Data · 5-Part Series May 2026
Published ~45 min · 5 Jupyter notebooks

What FAIR Data Actually Means for AI-Ready Pharmaceutical R&D

A practitioner's deep-dive into FAIR data maturity frameworks — moving beyond checklists to automated assessment, gap analysis, and an agentic scoring architecture. Combines two complementary models: the RDA FAIR Maturity Model (41 indicators, Essential / Important / Useful priority tiers) and the Pistoia Alliance FAIR Maturity Matrix (the organisational model: 6 levels L0–L5 × 7 dimensions, from “Life is unFAIR” to “FAIRest of them all”). Includes a complete manual assessment walkthrough on a CAR-T viability dataset, a prioritised remediation roadmap, and a semi-automated Python scorer built from the Zenodo API — the foundation for a forthcoming LangGraph agentic FAIR assessor.

Topics: RDA 41 indicators · Pistoia Alliance FMM v1.1 (L0–L5 × 7 dimensions) · Manual FAIR assessment · Gap analysis · Semi-automated scoring · Agentic FAIR scorer architecture
